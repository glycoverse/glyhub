# GPST000578 licensing evidence, 2026-10-14

## Result

The Springer-hosted Source Data XLS has no separate data-specific licence statement identified in its cells or the article data-availability statement. This remains unresolved, not a finding that numerical curation is prohibited.

The [article](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-73091-3#rightslink) is explicitly CC BY-NC-ND 4.0. Its notice limits the licensed material to noncommercial sharing with attribution and does not permit sharing adaptations under that licence. This review does not determine whether this numerical extraction exercises copyright, database rights, or constitutes an adaptation.

## Separate repository evidence

The article-linked [Panorama experiment](https://panoramaweb.org/Nglycopedia.url), [data DOI](https://doi.org/10.6069/rnan-xf90), explicitly displays “Data License: CC BY 4.0”. Its currently registered Skyline documents are CompleteNGlycoSRM.sky.zip (185 molecular precursors, 19 standard/isomer-library runs) and NGlycanDiscovery_Ovalbumin.sky.zip (three precursors, three runs). These are different assays from the nine selected Source Data XLS panels. A numerically matching repository export was not verified. Consequently the repository licence is recorded only for its deposited contents, not automatically assigned to the XLS matrices.

[GlycoPOST's terms](https://glycopost.glycosmos.org/terms) describe unrestricted use and redistribution of deposited data. Its separate website CC0 notice excludes user-submitted data. These terms do not by themselves establish terms for a publisher-hosted workbook.

## Publisher policies checked

- [Springer Nature research data policy](https://www.springernature.com/gp/authors/research-data-policy): requires transparent data-access statements; encourages availability, without supplying a blanket Source Data licence.
- [Data-policy FAQ](https://www.springernature.com/gp/authors/research-data-policy/data-policy-faqs): discusses sharing, access restrictions and citation; no universal dataset licence was found.
- [Nature Portfolio reporting standards](https://www.nature.com/nature-portfolio/editorial-policies/reporting-standards): distinguishes access obligations and disclosure of restrictions; does not establish a universal licence for accompanying numeric data.
- [Nature Communications source-data instructions](https://www.nature.com/ncomms/submit/how-to-submit#data-and-code): define numerical source-data submission formats, without stating a blanket licence for all Source Data files.

The same general-policy finding applies to GPST000602, DOI 10.1038/s41467-026-70850-0: those policies do not themselves establish data-specific terms for its supplementary quantitative tables. This is not a replacement for file- or deposit-specific evidence.

## Relevant official licence guidance

The [Creative Commons FAQ](https://creativecommons.org/faq/#which-components-of-databases-are-protected-by-copyright) distinguishes factual contents, arrangement, and applicable database rights. The [BY-NC-ND 4.0 legal code](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-nd/4.0/legalcode.en), sections 2(a)(2), 2(a)(4) and 4, addresses exceptions, technical format modifications and database rights. These sources caution against treating an article's ND label as an automatic determination about factual numerical extraction. No legal conclusion is made here.
